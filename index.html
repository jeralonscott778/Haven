    What should I bring to my appointment?

    Your hospital card if you have one, your NHIS or insurance card, any medicines you are taking, and any letters or test results from another clinic.

    Do you accept NHIS and private insurance?

    Please bring your card and ask at the front desk when you arrive. They will tell you what is covered before you are seen.

    How can I pay?

    You can pay at the cashier by cash, mobile money or card. Ask for a receipt for every payment.

    Can a family member book or ask on my behalf?

    Yes. A son, daughter or carer can fill in any form on this page with the patient's name and their own phone number.
